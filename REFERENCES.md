# References and attribution

The restoration models are existing third-party research implementations. The thesis work currently concerns evaluation and motion-guidance sensitivity, not authorship of these architectures.

- [GyroDeblurNet: official implementation and dataset instructions](https://github.com/hmyang0727/GyroDeblurNet), used at commit `ff8beec5d0af6e8e132bbff1db19ce46247ce869`.
- [Restormer: official implementation](https://github.com/swz30/Restormer), used at commit `68dc6ac472db26f16361150cb7a96a1bc87da93f` with its motion-deblurring checkpoint.

Checkpoint hashes and software versions are recorded in `results/settings.json` and `results/environment.txt`. Please obtain third-party code, data and weights from their original sources and follow their respective terms.
